What we collect

How we use it

To provide the app: sign you in, store and show your records and reports, let your staff access what you allow, and generate the documents you request. We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads.

Where it is stored

On Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions). Data is encrypted in transit. Some preferences stay on your device.

Sharing

We share data only with Google Firebase as our service provider, and where required by law. Documents you share as PDF or image go where you send them.

Staff access

Business owners control what their staff can see. Staff can only access data allowed by their permissions.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Menu → Delete account) or see how to request deletion. Deleting an owner account permanently removes business records, staff logins and uploaded files.
